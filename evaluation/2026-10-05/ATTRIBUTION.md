## Scope

Source labels are paraphrased in the manifest; consult each article for its original figure captions and diagnostic context. Labels on contact sheets are outside the model input. Visual review establishes image suitability, not medical diagnosis or clinical ground truth. Reuse must preserve attribution and the applicable license for each source.
